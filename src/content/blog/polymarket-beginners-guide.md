Polymarket is the world's largest prediction market by volume, but it requires a few steps that are unfamiliar if you have not used crypto before. This guide walks through the process from zero to your first trade.

**Important: Polymarket is not available to US persons.** If you are in the United States, use Kalshi instead. See our [Kalshi how-to guide](/guides/how-to-use-kalshi).

*Educational content only, not financial advice.*

## What you need before you start

- A computer or smartphone
- An email address
- A way to buy USDC (a US dollar-pegged stablecoin): explained below
- Some patience for the first-time setup

## Step 1: Get a crypto wallet

Polymarket supports several wallet options. The easiest for beginners is to use Polymarket's built-in embedded wallet, which is created automatically when you sign up with email or Google. You do not need to install MetaMask or manage seed phrases for this option.

If you already have a self-custody wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.), you can connect that instead.

## Step 2: Create your Polymarket account

Go to polymarket.com and click Sign Up. You can register with:
- Email and password
- Google account
- A connected crypto wallet

Complete any identity verification steps if prompted. Availability and verification requirements vary by region.

## Step 3: Get USDC

USDC is the dollar-pegged stablecoin used on Polymarket. 1 USDC = approximately $1.

Ways to get USDC:
- **Buy on a crypto exchange** (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, etc.) using bank transfer or card, then withdraw to your Polymarket wallet address
- **Use Polymarket's on-ramp**: the platform has partnerships with services like Moonpay that let you buy USDC directly with a card (fees apply)
- **Bridge from another network**: if you already hold assets on Ethereum mainnet, you can bridge to Polygon

For beginners, buying USDC on a reputable exchange and withdrawing to Polygon is usually the most cost-effective route, though it has a few steps.

## Step 4: Deposit into Polymarket

Once you have USDC in a wallet on the Polygon network, depositing to Polymarket is simple. In your Polymarket account, go to your balance and copy your deposit address. Send USDC from your wallet to that address.

Transfers on Polygon confirm in seconds and cost fractions of a cent.

## Step 5: Find a market

Browse the markets on Polymarket's home page or search for a topic you follow. Each market shows:

- The question (e.g. "Will the ECB cut rates in July?")
- The current price for Yes and No
- Total volume traded
- Resolution date and source

Click on a market to see more detail, including the full resolution criteria. Read these before trading.

## Step 6: Place your first trade

Once you have found a market you want to trade:

1. Choose Yes or No
2. Enter the number of contracts (each contract = $1 at resolution)
3. Choose market order (fills immediately at current price) or limit order (fills only at your specified price or better)
4. Review the confirmation screen showing your potential profit and loss
5. Confirm the trade

Your position appears in your portfolio immediately.

## Step 7: Monitor and exit

You do not have to hold until resolution. If the price moves in your favour, you can sell your contracts at a profit before the event resolves. If new information changes your view, you can cut the position at a loss.

Selling works the same way as buying: find your position, click Sell, and confirm.

## Common questions

**How long until I get paid?** When the market resolves, winnings are credited to your USDC balance automatically. Time varies by event.

**What if I disagree with how a market resolved?** Polymarket uses UMA's oracle for resolution. If resolution is contested, a dispute mechanism is available. Read the resolution criteria and dispute process on any market you trade.

**Can I withdraw whenever I want?** Yes. Withdraw USDC to your wallet at any time. Converting back to fiat requires a crypto exchange.

**What is the minimum trade size?** Check Polymarket's current minimums: they are generally small (a few dollars or less).

## Tips for your first trades

- Start with a small amount (under $20) to learn the mechanics before committing more
- Choose a high-volume market where spreads are tight
- Read the resolution criteria carefully before buying
- Do not chase a price that just moved sharply on news: you may be buying last
