Polymarket is the world's largest prediction market by trading volume, built on the Polygon blockchain. It has become the go-to platform for political and current events trading, attracting serious forecasters and casual traders alike with deep liquidity and a wide range of markets.

*This article is for education only and is not financial advice.*

## What is Polymarket?

Founded in 2020, Polymarket runs on decentralized infrastructure. Trades are settled by smart contracts on Polygon (an Ethereum sidechain), and payouts are in USDC: a USD-pegged stablecoin. Because it operates on-chain, it does not require a traditional brokerage account, but it does require a crypto wallet and USDC to get started.

## Who can use it?

Polymarket is **not available to US persons** due to regulatory restrictions: the CFTC has taken enforcement action against the platform in the past, and US-based access is blocked. If you are outside the US and your jurisdiction permits it, you can generally sign up and trade.

Always check whether prediction market trading is legal in your country before depositing funds.

## What can you trade?

Polymarket has one of the broadest market selections of any platform:

- **Politics**: elections worldwide, geopolitical events, policy decisions
- **Crypto**: ETF approvals, protocol launches, price milestones
- **Finance**: central bank decisions, macro data releases
- **Science and tech**: AI milestones, product launches, research outcomes
- **Culture and sport**: award shows, championships, media events

Many markets attract tens of millions of dollars in volume, giving them some of the tightest spreads and best liquidity in the prediction market space.

## How does trading work?

Polymarket uses an **automated market maker (AMM)** for most markets: you trade against a liquidity pool rather than a live order book. This means you can always get a fill, but large orders move the price. Some markets also have an order book layer for more precise limit trading.

Contracts pay out at $1 USDC for correct outcomes and $0 for incorrect ones. Resolution is handled by UMA, an optimistic oracle protocol that uses a dispute mechanism if resolution is contested.

## Fees

Trading fees on Polymarket are generally low: around 1–2% depending on the market and trade size. There are no deposit fees, but you will pay standard Polygon network gas fees (typically very small). Withdrawing USDC back to a bank account requires converting through a crypto exchange, which adds steps and potential fees.

## Who is Polymarket best for?

- **Non-US traders** who want the deepest liquidity and largest market selection
- **Crypto-comfortable users** already holding USDC or comfortable with wallets
- **Serious forecasters** who want to trade real-world events at scale
- **Those interested in global politics** and current events beyond the US

## Who might prefer something else?

- **US residents**: access is blocked
- **Crypto newcomers**: setting up a wallet and acquiring USDC has friction for first-timers
- **Those wanting USD deposits**: there is no direct fiat on-ramp; you need crypto first

## Bottom line

Polymarket's combination of deep liquidity, wide market selection, and on-chain settlement makes it the most powerful prediction market currently available. The barriers are the crypto requirement and US access restriction. If you are outside the US and comfortable with wallets, it is hard to beat for serious forecasting.

*Availability varies by country. US persons may not use Polymarket. Always verify your eligibility and local legal status before creating an account.*
